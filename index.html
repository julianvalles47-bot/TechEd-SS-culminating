G7A Social Studies Culminating Project

For our Social Studies culminating, 3 subjects were integrated: Performing Arts, Visual Arts, and Tech Ed. For all three of these, we made a project related to our Social Studies project.


Performing Arts

For PA, we had to make an original song based on things we learned in Vietnam or our SS classes. Our main theme was "kapayapaan", peace. Three groups had to make an original song using a backing track or by making their own song.



Visual Arts

For VA, we had to make a contemporary artwork. Our artworks were based on our place in our community, and we showed them through 3D sculpture made out of various materials.



Social Studies

For Social Studies, we made a presentation analyzing a certain conflict common in schools. Each group presented a conflict, statistics, real-life case, and for some groups, even a short skit to actually show the conflict.



Tech Ed

And finally, we made a website to tie all of these together.
