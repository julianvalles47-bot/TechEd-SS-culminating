SOCIAL STUDIES

Our performance task in Social Studies was to address conflicts that happen in different communities, using different materials that could prevent and help with the current conflicts. Students are able to create infographics, brochures, toolkits, and more, in order to address their chosen conflicts, giving details and giving out ways to prevent and solve the conflicts chosen by the students.
