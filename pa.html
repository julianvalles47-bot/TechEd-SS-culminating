PERFORMING ARTS

Our performance task in Performing Arts was to make a song about peace from the Vietnam war. We were tasked to create a song using Filipino, with our main theme of ‘Kapayapaan’. Groups used backing tracks, or even made their own music. Each group was tasked ti make their own lyrics and melody, in order to create the entire song.
