PERFORMING ARTS

Our performance task in Performing Arts was to make a song about peace from the Vietnam war. We were tasked to create a song using Filipino, with our main theme of ‘Kapayapaan’. Groups used backing tracks, or even made their own music. Each group was tasked to make their own lyrics and melody, in order to create the entire song.


(Ellie, Nate, Lucas, Anton, Belly)

Description

Our song is based on one central theme, kapayapaan (or peace in english). But we speak from a perspective of people who are trying to shine light on the reasons why we can’t achieve peace. We’re making a stand to speak our message, which is about how everyone is separated by ideas, identity, differences, instead of accepting the difference. And thats why we can’t have peace in the world. We made this song by delegating all the different parts, and then unifying and changing them to fit and connect with all of our points. We really wrote this song with the intention to make it a sort of “wake up” call to the fact that since communities and people separate because of differences instead of accepting and living alongside it; we make our lives harder.

Description

Our song centers on the aftermath of war. When talking about war, people often discuss what happened in the war itself, like the destruction, fighting, and many others. However, the conflict doesn't end when the war does. It persists in the form of political disputes, infrastructure damage, economical slumps, PTSD, and more. Our song highlights a few of those, including some of the things that happen during war itself. We wrote this song with this theme in mind, and split it into two parts: the first part is what it's like during the war, and the second is the aftermath (which we wanted to focus on). We included examples like dissilussionment after war, trauma, and destruction. We also showed that things like this will keep on happening unless we have peace, or kapayapaan, which is our overall theme.
